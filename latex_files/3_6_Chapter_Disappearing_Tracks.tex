\section{Preface}

The work presented in this thesis that contributes to this search focuses primarily on the addition of new signal models. In particular, the \stau GMSB and \stau co annihilation models as described in Sec.~\ref{sec:SUSY}. 


For completeness, the full published paper is presented in this chapter.
