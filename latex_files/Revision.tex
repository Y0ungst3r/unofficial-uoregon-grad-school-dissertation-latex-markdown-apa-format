Integration of the Inner System proceeds as follows. 
Detector modules are received at SLAC and undergo a series of electrical and visual inspections to verify full functionality following shipment. 
Once qualified, the modules are loaded onto the local mechanical supports by bonding them to the carbon-fiber laminate using the thermally conductive compound SE4445.

After loading, the local supports are placed in a dry box and cooled to the operating temperature of \(-55^\circ\)C. 
While at this temperature, the loaded supports undergo a comprehensive set of electrical tests to ensure that all modules operate correctly under nominal detector conditions.

Following successful cold testing, the local supports are mounted into their corresponding shell structures. 
The titanium cooling pipes integrated within the supports are then connected via orbital welding to form a continuous cooling circuit. 
Once fully assembled, the completed shell structures are prepared for shipment and transported to CERN for final installation in the ATLAS detector.
